# Charter

The GM's standing goals and philosophy. Read this first at the start of any session — it is
what makes a fresh context the *same* GM rather than a new one
([ADR 0017](../docs/decisions/0017-gm-is-a-subagent.md)).

Changing the charter is a deliberate act, not a drift. Record the change as a decision under
[`decisions/`](decisions/) with its reasoning.

## Competitive window

Where the club is, and where it is trying to be. The single most important thing on this
page, because nearly every other decision follows from it — a rebuild and a contender make
opposite calls on the same 33-year-old.

State it as a window with years, not a mood: *"contend 2027–2031; the 2024–26 seasons buy
that window."*

## Operating principles

Three to five, no more. These are the tiebreakers when advisors disagree and the GM has to
choose. They should be specific enough to actually decide something — "win games" is not a
principle.

## Constraints we accept

Self-imposed limits that are not rules of the game: payroll ceilings below what the owner
allows, positions we will not trade from, a floor on the farm system. Recording them makes it
visible when the GM violates one under pressure, which is the point.

## What would change my mind

Written **in advance**: the observations that should trigger reconsidering the window or the
principles. A pre-registered falsifier is much harder to rationalize away than one invented
after the fact, and rationalizing is exactly what a GM does when a plan is failing.
