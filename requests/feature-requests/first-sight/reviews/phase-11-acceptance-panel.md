## Confirmed findings (major)

### CF-01 — "78 of 89 declared fields reach a page" is false â€” 23 declared-and-renderable fields are bound to no column, reach no page, and are named in no withheld section

**Where:** `src/ootp_ai/catalog/structure.py:254-257 (rendered at docs/warehouse-catalog.md:11 and docs/warehouse-catalog.json:2-5)`

**Problem.** `Structure.field_count` is `len(contracts.fields)` and `served_field_count` counts every field whose POLICY DISPOSITION is RENDERABLE; neither asks whether the field is bound to a column of a declared table. I re-derived this from the shipped declaration myself rather than trusting the panel: declared 89, distinct field names referenced by any `[[table.column]]` 57, policy-renderable over the whole declaration 78, bound AND renderable 55, unbound-but-counted-as-served 23. The headline sentence therefore overstates what is served by 23 fields, and its second clause ('the rest are named under *What is withheld* below') is false for every one of them â€” they are in neither the served surface nor the 11-row withheld table. The same wrong numbers go into the machine-readable half. The page contradicts itself: :71, generated from the declaration's own coverage note, says `second_nation_id`, the two language ids, the nickname index, `loan_league_id` and `historical_team_id` are entries 'the walker verified and chose not to surface' â€” all six are inside the 78. Unbound entries I confirmed include `content_digest` (a property of the file, not a column), `tail_mask`, `calendar_flag_typing`, `name_table_save_ownership`, `names_declared_record_count`. This is the one number a GM would use to price how much of the warehouse it is actually seeing, on the artifact whose stated purpose is exactly that, and it errs toward understating the gap.

**Proposed fix.** In `build_structure`, derive `bound = {c.field for t in contracts.tables for c in t.columns if c.field}` and compute `served_field_count = sum(1 for e in contracts.fields if e.name in bound and disposition(e) is Disposition.RENDERABLE)` â€” 55 today. Then surface the third category instead of losing it: add an 'unexposed' count and a short table of the 23 declared-but-unbound renderable fields under *What is withheld*, since a field the walker verified and deliberately did not surface is precisely a gap the GM should be able to price. Add an offline assertion in tests/test_catalog.py that `served_field_count` equals the bound-and-renderable count, so the two definitions cannot drift apart again.

### CF-02 — The AC15 player-value leak guard samples 500 consecutive names from one alphabetical band, and 29 real landed names appear verbatim on the page just outside that band

**Where:** `tests/test_catalog.py:549-557 (sample query) and :573-578 (assertion)`

**Problem.** Raised by four lenses independently and confirmed by two verify passes. The query is `SELECT name_text ... AND CHAR_LENGTH(name_text) >= 5 LIMIT 500` with no ORDER BY. Measured against the live landing: the eligible population is 247,583 rows (239,263 distinct) and the sample is 500 consecutive names Aalto..Akrodhana spanning only the initials Aa..Ak â€” 0.2% of one alphabetical neighbourhood. It is an assertion about names beginning with two letters, not about the page. Worse, it is a false-red landmine: substring-testing the FULL distinct population against the rendered page yields 29 collisions â€” Boston, Chall/Challe/Challen, Cover, Every, Field, Flagg, Gener/Genera/General, Grain, Group, Label, Lahman, Landi/Landin/Landing, Manager, Mappin, Parse, Perio, Rende/Render, Roster, Table, Walke, Walker â€” of which six are emitted unconditionally by the renderer on every table block ('- **Walker** â€”' render.py:222, '- **Grain** â€”' :219, the '| Field | Source | ...' withheld header :317, 'Every figure...' :279). `sample âˆ© collisions` is empty today purely by accident of physical row order. Any ORDER BY, LIMIT widening, index change, MySQL plan change or a different save's name table turns this red on a completely correct catalog â€” the failure mode IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1065 and Â§4.3 name twice as the most expensive kind of wrong test. MIN_SAMPLED_NAME_LEN = 5 (tests/test_catalog.py:87) is measurably too short: 'Every', 'Field', 'Group', 'Label' are 5-6 characters. The player-id half is sound but incidentally so â€” row counts render with thousands separators, so `\b<id>\b` cannot match; a full sweep of all 21,344 ids >= 10,000 found zero collisions.

**Proposed fix.** Stop substring-matching lone name-table tokens against English prose â€” the whole-population result shows the collision is structural, not a sampling accident. Sample the names actually RESOLVED onto landed players (join bronze_player's two name indices to bronze_name) as composite 'First Last' display strings, which cannot collide with prose by coincidence, and spread the sample deterministically (ORDER BY name_index with a modulus) so it covers the whole table. Keep the existing `assert names and ids` anti-vacuity line and add one asserting the sample spans more than one initial letter, plus a self-check that the sample contains at least one known-colliding token so the assertion is proven to have teeth.
